# Privacy Policy for Hear the Band

**Last updated:** September 19, 2026  
**Effective date:** September 19, 2026

Hear the Band is an Android ear-training app developed by Nicolas Broguiere. This policy explains what the app stores, when data may leave your device, and how you can manage it. We do not provide user accounts, advertising, or analytics.

---

## 1. At a glance

| Topic | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Practice data | Progress, answer history, custom exercises, and settings are stored in the app's local storage. |
| Purchases | Google Play handles the optional full-version purchase. The app sends Google Play a randomly generated, persistent installation identifier when starting a purchase. |
| Backup | Android may back up eligible app data to your Google account or transfer it to another device, depending on your device settings. |
| Sharing | You choose when to share an exercise or export a progress backup, and which app or storage location receives it. |
| Permissions | The app requests internet and network-state access for Google Play Billing. It does not request microphone, location, or contacts access. |

---

## 2. Data stored on your device

Hear the Band stores the following in app preferences and files on your device:

- Exercise results and answer history, for progress tracking and adaptive questions.
- Saved or imported exercises and app and audio preferences, for your training and settings.
- A local purchase record, for offline access to purchased features.

We do not operate a server that receives your practice data.

The app does not include an advertising or analytics SDK. It does not record microphone audio or access your contacts or location.

---

## 3. Google Play purchases

The optional one-time full-version purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not receive your card number, banking details, or billing address. The app checks purchase status through Google Play and keeps a signed purchase record locally for offline access. Purchase information, including a purchase token, is exchanged with Google Play as part of billing.

When you start a purchase, the app sends Google Play an obfuscated account ID: a SHA-256 hash of a random value generated for this installation and saved locally. It is not derived from your name or email address, but it can distinguish this installation from others. Google's handling of billing data is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The app uses `INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` for billing and connectivity checks. Opening a support email or external link uses the app you choose on your device; information you send through that app is handled by its provider.

---

## 4. Android backup and device transfer

Android backup is enabled for Hear the Band. Depending on your device and backup settings, Android may copy eligible app preferences, progress, and saved exercises to a backup associated with your Google account or transfer them to another device. The app excludes its billing preferences and demo-access preferences from these backups and transfers. We do not have access to your Android backups. See [Google's Android backup information](https://support.google.com/android/answer/2819582) for backup controls and deletion.

---

## 5. Sharing and export

You can share a saved exercise through Android's share sheet as a `.jete` file, which contains JSON exercise data. The recipient app you select can receive that file. You can also export a progress backup as a `.htb` file to a location you select and later import it. A progress backup can contain practice history and saved exercises. These actions start only when you choose them. Copies you share or export are governed by the recipient app or storage provider and remain there until you delete them separately.

---

## 6. Retention and deletion

The app keeps its local data until you clear its storage in Android Settings or uninstall it. Clearing app storage also removes local purchase records; Google Play can restore a valid purchase. Uninstalling or clearing the app does not necessarily remove Android backups, device-transfer copies, or files you exported or shared. Manage those copies through your Google account, the destination app, or the storage location you selected.

You can export a progress backup before clearing storage or moving to another device. For privacy questions or requests concerning information you sent us directly, contact us using the address below.

---

## 7. Children and privacy rights

Hear the Band is not specifically directed at children. The app has no account system and we do not operate a database of users' practice records. You can access, export, and erase app data on your device as described above. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include in your message and use it to respond. You may write to us about access, correction, or deletion of that correspondence.

---

## 8. Security and changes

Purchase records are checked locally using Google Play signatures. Exercise and progress data is stored in Android app storage, subject to your device's security and backup settings. No method of storage or transfer is guaranteed to be completely secure.

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The updated version will show a new date at the top.
